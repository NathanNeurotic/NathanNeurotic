About

I'm Nathan. Online I go by NathanNeurotic, or Ripto.

Most of my time goes into PlayStation 2 homebrew: maintaining loaders and launchers, fixing bugs, wiring up support for new storage and network setups, and writing documentation so people don't have to dig through forum threads to get things working. I also make pictures and music, and I get far too invested in ideas that start with "wouldn't it be funny if…". Sometimes they turn into finished projects.

I share work as I go, including the parts that don't work yet. If I've got something wrong, tell me. A better idea is a better idea, whoever it comes from. I'll use whatever gets a thing working: my own code, what the community already knows, or AI tools. The art and music on this site are AI-made, and they're labeled that way.

Neuralotics

Neuralotics is the group around my projects, on Discord and Telegram. People bring whatever they're working on, whether that's code, hardware, art or a half-built experiment. We test things, question them, and give credit to whoever helped.
