About

I'm Nathan. Online I go by NathanNeurotic, or Ripto.

Most of my time goes into PlayStation 2 homebrew: fixing bugs, adding support for new storage and network setups, and writing the docs I wish I'd had, so nobody has to dig through ten-year-old forum threads to get a game running. I also make pictures and music, and I get far too invested in ideas that start with "wouldn't it be funny if…". Sometimes they even turn into finished projects.

I share things while they're still half-built, broken bits included. If I've got something wrong, tell me. A better idea is a better idea, whoever has it, and I'll use whatever gets us there: my own code, what the community already knows, or AI tools.

Neuralotics

Neuralotics is the crew around my projects, on Discord and Telegram. Bring whatever you're working on: code, hardware, art or a half-built experiment. We test things, poke holes in them, and give credit to whoever helped.
